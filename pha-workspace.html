Please read before use

Evaluation beta. Not an engineering sign-off.

PHA Workspace is experimental software for exploration and pilot studies. It does not certify Process Hazard Analyses, validate operating limits, qualify safeguards or risk reductions, or replace a qualified multidisciplinary PHA team. Independently verify all drawings, calculations, scenarios, and reports against approved procedures, engineering evidence, and regulatory requirements.

  • Protect your work. IndexedDB is browser-managed working storage. Export dated .phaz backups and test recovery; clearing browser data or changing profiles can result in lost work.
  • Protect your drawings. Only import proprietary information on equipment and computers where you are authorized to handle it. No cloud or server is required for this application.
  • Know the limits. Large PDFs may affect performance; print formatting and storage behavior depend on your browser. No multiuser synchronization, formal approvals, or independent safeguard-credit engine are provided in v0.5.0.
  • As-is beta. No warranty of accuracy, completeness, reliability, fitness or availability is offered to the extent permitted by law. Use at your own discretion and with qualified review.
