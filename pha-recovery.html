01 / THE PROBLEM

Don't pay to rediscover what your team already knew.

A PDF can preserve a finished PHA without making its underlying scenarios easy to search, compare, or edit. RDS aims to bridge that gap without asking you to recreate the original engineering work.

01 / THE RECORD

Years of technical history.

Previous teams identified causes, consequences, safeguards, and recommended actions. That record may be scattered across decades of reports.

03 / THE OPPORTUNITY

Structured knowledge again.

Recover selected historical content or prepare a verified editable project—with clear source references and documented gaps.
